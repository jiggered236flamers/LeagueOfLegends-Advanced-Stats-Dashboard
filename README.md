## Key Features

The League of Legends Advanced Stats Dashboard offers a range of features tailored to enhance your gaming sessions:

1. **Live Stats Overlay**: View real-time metrics such as kills, deaths, assists, creep score, and gold per minute while in-game. The overlay is fully customizable in terms of position, size, and opacity, allowing you to focus on what matters most.

2. **Match History Dashboard**: Analyze your past games with detailed graphs and statistics. See your performance trends over time, champion proficiency, and compare with friends to identify strengths and areas for improvement.

3. **Customizable Widgets**: Drag and drop different widgets on your desktop to create a personalized stat display. Choose from a variety of themes and layouts to match your style and preferences.

4. **Performance Analytics**: Receive AI-powered insights and recommendations based on your playstyle. Identify patterns, track your progress, and get tips to enhance your skills in League of Legends.

5. **Seamless Integration**: The tool runs in the background without interfering with the game. It uses official APIs to ensure data accuracy and security, providing a smooth and reliable experience.
